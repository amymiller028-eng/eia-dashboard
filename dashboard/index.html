Where these numbers come from

Source: the EQ Trends raw data file (Emotional Intelligence Appraisal, from the October 26, 2026 data snapshot). Everything outside Retests uses self-assessments from 2021 through September 2026 with all 28 questions answered, the same group as the EQ trends research pages. Scores are self-ratings on a 0–100 scale; low is below 65 and high is above 85.

Third-Party Research Facts are written by the dashboard's editors from published studies; each card carries its full citation.

Retests compare each person's first self-assessment with their first retest (2021–2026) at least 30 days later; the retest rate counts every retest for people who first took it in 2021–2025. Generation is estimated from the age band and the year of the assessment.
